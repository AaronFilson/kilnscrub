# US National Code Compliance Audit (`kilnscrub`)

**Scope:** Independent code-compliance audit of `kilnscrub`'s studio readiness calculators, COTS & fabrication Bills of Materials (BOMs), build/operations guides, safety limits, and Modbus control logic against US national model codes and federal regulations:

- **NEC (NFPA 70)** — Electrical (Articles 110, 210, 400, 430)
- **IMC (International Mechanical Code)** — Mechanical Exhaust & Hazardous Exhaust (Sections 401, 501, 510, Chapter 8)
- **NFPA 91 / NFPA 211 / NFPA 86** — Industrial Exhaust Systems, High-Heat Chimneys & Clearances, Ovens & Furnaces Safety Interlocks
- **IPC (International Plumbing Code) & 40 CFR Part 403** — Potable Water Backflow Protection, Wastewater Temperature/Neutralization, Federal POTW Pretreatment
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1000 & 1910.95** — Workplace Air Contaminants (HCl Ceiling PEL) & Occupational Noise Exposure

**Audited Codebase Files:**
- `pkg/studio/urban.go`, `pkg/studio/cots.go`, `pkg/studio/guide.go`, `pkg/studio/presets.go`
- `pkg/cad/bom.go`
- `pkg/model/types.go`
- `internal/control/interlock.go`, `internal/control/modbus_hal.go`, `internal/control/controller.go`
- `configs/kilnscrub.example.json`
- Reference physics baseline: `docs/ENGINEERING_VALIDATION.md` (post-A1–A4 fix: 24 cu.ft. wood/salt kiln at `2400°F`, `2.0 lbs/hr NaCl`, `183.9°F` quench saturation, `10.0 ppm` outlet HCl, `21.21 inWC` static pressure, `1.38 shaft HP` → **`2.0 HP` motor**; 12 cu.ft. `18.5 ppm` HCl; 48 cu.ft. `3.0 HP` motor).

> **Disclaimer:** Model codes (NEC, IMC, IPC, NFPA) are adopted with state and municipal amendments. Final permitting, electrical/mechanical/plumbing inspections, and sewer/air-district approvals remain under the local Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) and Publicly Owned Treatment Works (POTW).

## Executive Summary

While `kilnscrub`'s thermodynamic and scrubber physics kernel is rigorously validated (`docs/ENGINEERING_VALIDATION.md`), this code-compliance audit identified **14 concrete code-compliance and safety gaps** across the studio calculator, COTS/CAD BOMs, build guides, and control HAL — including **5 Critical** issues that must be resolved in Phase 6 integration (`integrator-p6`):

1. **Critical (NEC 430.122 / 430.22 / Table 430.248):** `pkg/studio/urban.go:145-198` under-calculates single-phase VFD input current by **40%–100%** and omits the mandatory **125% NEC conductor factor**, sizing a `1.0 HP` 120V VFD on a `20A` circuit (requires `30A`), a `2.0 HP` 240V VFD on a `20A` circuit (requires `30A`), and a `3.0 HP` 240V VFD on a `20A` circuit (requires `40A`). It also omits the plume diluter amp draw (`6.5–12.5 A`), NEC `430.102` lockable sight disconnect, and NEC `210.8(F)` VFD/GFCI nuisance-tripping engineering notes.
2. **Critical (NEC 110.28 / Enclosure Mismatch):** `pkg/studio/cots.go:395-425` cites truncated/non-existent VFD SKUs (`GS21-10P`, `GS21-20P`) while claiming `"NEMA 4X VFD"` at IP20 open-chassis pricing; standard AutomationDirect `GS21` drives are **IP20 / NEMA 1** and require either a **NEMA 4X enclosure** or the washdown **GS21X (NEMA 4X / IP66)** series around a wet HCl scrubber.
3. **Critical (IPC Section 608):** `pkg/studio/urban.go:200-230`, `pkg/studio/guide.go:158, 188`, and `pkg/studio/cots.go` instruct hooking a potable garden hose directly to the chemical scrubber quench manifold and sump float valve with **zero backflow prevention** — a High-Hazard cross-connection requiring an **engineered Air Gap (IPC 608.15.1)** or certified **ASSE 1013 RPZ assembly**.
4. **Critical (IPC Section 702.5 & 803.1 / 40 CFR 403.5):** `pkg/studio/guide.go:202, 222` instructs cracking live `0.08 GPM` blowdown during firing (`~184°F` sump liquor) and draining after 1–2 hours without verifying temperature **`<= 140°F`**, violating **IPC 702.5** (`140°F` PVC/ABS sanitary drain ceiling) and risking melted residential DWV piping.
5. **Critical (NFPA 86 / Fail-Safe Control & Thermal Limits):**
   - `pkg/model/types.go:456` and `configs/kilnscrub.example.json:30` default `MaxQuenchOutletTemp` to **`210°F`**, contradicting `pkg/studio/cots.go:262` (`ASTM F441 CPVC, 200°F max`) and `cots.go:240` (`55-gal HDPE drum`, unsafe at `184°F` under `-20 inWC` suction vacuum!).
   - `internal/control/modbus_hal.go:368, 402` drives `BypassDamperCoilAddr` as **energize-to-open (`true`)** instead of **de-energize-to-trip** with a hardware watchdog, and `guide.go:61` erroneously recommends **`650°F` RTV silicone** on a **`2400°F`** dry kiln flue!

### 2.2 Audit Findings in `urban.go`, `cots.go`, `guide.go`, `bom.go`, `types.go`, and `control/`

1. **Missing IMC 501.3.1 / 510 Setbacks & Misleading `"Harmless Water Vapor"` Claim (`pkg/studio/urban.go:315-320, 427-448`, `pkg/studio/presets.go:132, 306`):**
   - `assessPlume` sets `MinStackHeightFt` to `10–15 ft` but never checks **30 ft property-line setback for `VaporGlazeSalt` (IMC 501.3.1 Item 1)** or **10 ft minimum for non-salt (Item 2)** — `presets.go:306` even allows `PropertyLineFt = 3.0 ft` without raising an IMC `action_needed` flag!
   - `urban.go:427` states `"Exhaust is harmless 184 F water vapor (NOT kiln smoke)"` unconditionally, even when `VaporGlazeMode == VaporGlazeSalt` produces **`10–18.5 ppm HCl`** at the stack exit.
   - Neither `urban.go` nor `guide.go` states the mandatory **10 ft setback from operable windows/doors and outdoor air intakes** or **6 ft / 3 ft wall/roof clearance** (IMC 501.3.1).
2. **Dangerous `650°F` RTV Silicone Recommendation on a `2400°F` Flue (`pkg/studio/guide.go:61`):**
   - `guide.go:61` instructs: `"high-temp RTV silicone (rated 650 F) for dry flue joints"`. Upstream of Stage 1 Quench, dry kiln flue gas is **`1830°F–2400°F`** (`cots.go:722` also lists `"High-temp silicone/PTFE flue gaskets"`). Silicone thermally decomposes/pyrolyzes above `600°F` and PTFE decomposes above `500°F` (emitting toxic perfluoroisobutylene fumes!). Hot dry flue joints upstream of Stage 1 **must** use **`2300°F–2700°F` alumina-silica ceramic fiber rope/gaskets or refractory mortar**; PTFE/EPDM gaskets belong strictly downstream of Stage 1 (`<= 200°F`).
3. **Missing NFPA 211 `36"` Clearances, Bypass Chimney BOM Item, & NFPA 91 Cleanouts (`guide.go:85-95, 139-145`, `cots.go`, `bom.go`):**
   - `guide.go:140` mentions `"an auxiliary heat-proof bypass chimney"` in passing, but neither `cots.go` nor `pkg/cad/bom.go` includes the hot-flue refractory insulation, bypass tee/chimney stack, or NFPA 91 duct cleanout hatches.
4. **CPVC (`200°F`) & HDPE (`140–160°F` Vacuum) Thermal/Structural Mismatch (`pkg/model/types.go:456`, `pkg/studio/cots.go:229-241, 262-266`, `configs/kilnscrub.example.json:30`):**
   - `cots.go:262` states `"Schedule 40/80 CPVC (ASTM F441, 200 F max)"` while `cots.go:266` and `types.go:456` set the safety trip at **`210°F`** — `10°F` *above* CPVC's continuous rating! Lower default `MaxQuenchOutletTemp` to **`200°F` (`366.48 K`)** (with warning alarm at `195°F`).
   - `cots.go:229-241` selects stacked **55-gal open-head HDPE drums** for `16"–22.5"` packed columns, noting `"keep quench <= 185 F"`. However, Stage 3 operates at **`183.9°F` AND under `-19 to -21 inWC` (`-0.7 psig`) induced-draft suction vacuum** downstream of the venturi! Standard thin-walled HDPE drums lose modulus sharply above `140–160°F` and risk **vacuum buckling/implosion** at `184°F` under `-20 inWC` negative pressure. Replace stacked HDPE drums with **Schedule 40/80 CPVC**, **vinyl-ester FRP** (ASTM E84 Class 1), or require internal vacuum-stiffening rings + upstream dilution air (`T_sat <= 150°F`).
5. **Galvanized Steel Mixing Tee in Salt-HCl Exhaust (`pkg/studio/cots.go:494`):**
   - `cots.go:494` specifies `"Galvanized/316L Stack Mixing Tee & Taper Cone"` for the plume diluter. In `VaporGlazeSalt` mode (`10–18.5 ppm HCl` + wet condensate), HCl rapidly strips galvanized zinc coatings (`Zn + 2HCl -> ZnCl2 + H2`). Require **CPVC / FRP / 316L** for the wetted stack mixing tee and cone when `VaporGlazeMode == VaporGlazeSalt` (galvanized steel is only acceptable on the dry ambient-air intake side of the dilution blower).
6. **NFPA 86 Interlock & Fail-Safe Polarity Defects (`internal/control/modbus_hal.go:368, 402`, `internal/control/interlock.go:142-170`, `configs/kilnscrub.example.json`):**
   - **Energize-to-Trip Polarity (`modbus_hal.go:368, 402`):** Comment says `"energize/open bypass damper"` and writes `true` (`0xFF00`) to `BypassDamperCoilAddr` on trip. If a field relay coil is wired energize-to-open, then **RS-485 bus failure, Pi crash, or relay module power loss leaves the damper CLOSED**! Add a `DeenergizeToTrip` / `FailSafeCoilActiveLow` boolean config option (default `true` for physical safety relays: coil `ON` = healthy hold-closed; coil `OFF` / power loss / watchdog timeout = spring-return **OPEN**) plus a **hardware watchdog heartbeat toggle**!
   - **Missing Exhaust-Proving & Positive-Draft Interlock (`interlock.go:164-168`):** `interlock.go` trips on `|DraftPa| > MaxDraftMagnitudePa` (`1.0 inWC`), but does **not** detect **loss of exhaust proving** (e.g., `BlowerSpeedFeedback < min` while VFD commanded active, or sustained positive firebox pressure `DraftPa > +0.05 inWC` indicating blower failure/choked venturi). Adding an optional exhaust-proving fault check strengthens NFPA 86 alignment (especially for gas-fired kilns).

## 3. IPC (International Plumbing Code) & 40 CFR Part 403 — Water Supply & Drain Disposal

### 3.1 Governing Code Requirements

| Code / Regulation | Exact Requirement | Applicability to `kilnscrub` |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IPC Section 608** *(Protection of Potable Water Supply — Table 608.1, 608.15.1, 608.15.4.2, 608.16)* | Protects drinking water from **backsiphonage and backpressure** cross-connections. **Section 608.15.4.2** requires hose bibbs/sillcocks to have backflow protection (ASSE 1011/1052 vacuum breaker — *backsiphonage only, not continuous pressure*). For **High-Hazard chemical process tanks/scrubbers** (HCl, NaCl, NaHCO₃, wood ash, ceramic glaze metals), **Table 608.1 / Section 608.16** mandates either: (1) an **Air Gap** (`>= 2×` supply pipe diameter, min `1 inch` vertical separation above the vessel's flood-level rim per **IPC 608.15.1 / ASME A112.1.2**), OR (2) a certified **ASSE 1013 Reduced Pressure Principle (RPZ) Backflow Prevention Assembly** (tested annually). | **Direct garden-hose connection to a pressurized quench nozzle manifold or submerged sump float valve is a severe code violation.** The safest, lowest-cost studio-compliant architecture is an **overhead Air Gap** filling a dedicated **make-up/break tank** (which feeds a dedicated booster/recirc pump), OR an **ASSE 1013 RPZ** upstream of any direct supply line. |
| **IPC Section 702.5** *(Temperature Rating of Sanitary Drainage Piping)* | *"Where the wastewater temperature will be **greater than 140°F (60°C)**, the sanitary drainage piping material shall be rated for the highest temperature of the wastewater."* Standard residential PVC and ABS DWV piping is rated to **`140°F` maximum**. | **Direct violation for live blowdown and hot sump drainage!** Scrubber sump liquor operates at **`183.9°F` (`84.4°C`)** during peak firing — **`44°F` above the IPC 702.5 limit**. Hot blowdown (`0.08 GPM`) or post-firing sump water MUST be cooled/tempered to **`<= 140°F`** (via a cooling catch tank or cold-water tempering valve/tee) before entering any PVC/ABS building drain. |
